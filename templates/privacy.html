Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Your expenses say a lot about your life, so we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it and how we keep it safe. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here and in our Terms and Conditions.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information: your name, email address and password when you register. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data: information such as your browser type and the time of your requests, which our server records automatically to keep the Service working.

We do not ask for or store bank account details, card numbers or UPI credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries, category breakdowns and date-range views.
  • Keep the Service secure and fix problems when something goes wrong.
  • Contact you about important changes to the Service or this policy.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and is only accessible through your account. We take reasonable technical steps to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse, but no system connected to the internet can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your account information and expense records, except where we are required by law to keep them.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares basic technical information such as your IP address. Google's use of that information is covered by its own privacy policy.

If we add other third-party services in future, we will update this policy to say what they are and what data they receive.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to access, correct or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
